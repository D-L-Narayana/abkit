A/B-test calculator & experimentation toolkit

The formulas below are the JavaScript twin of the Python package abkit: two-proportion z-test with confidence interval, sample-size planning, and the sample-ratio-mismatch guardrail. Underneath, the Monte-Carlo studies that check the statistics actually hold (A/A tests, power, peeking, CUPED, SRM, interleaving).

Two-proportion z-test

Control (A) vs treatment (B) conversion rates. Pooled SE for the statistic, Wald CI for the difference.

Sample size per arm

Visitors needed in each arm to detect a relative lift on a conversion rate (two-sided).

Sample-ratio mismatch (SRM)

Chi-square goodness of fit of arm sizes against the intended split. A p-value below 0.001 means the bucketing is broken — do not read the metrics.

Monte-Carlo validation (from abkit simulate, seed 2026)

Reproduce with pip install -e . && abkit simulate; raw output in reports/simulation.json.
